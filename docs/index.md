# What is the API3 DAO?

::: warning Legacy DAO

These pages document the legacy API3 DAO on Aragon. The
[DAO migration proposal](https://api3.eth.limo/#/history/primary-17) moved
governance to Snapshot, and the
[current DAO documentation](https://docs.api3.org/dao/) covers it.

:::

API3 delegates much of its governance to the vote of a Decentralized Autonomous
Organization (DAO). API3 is a collaborative effort to build, manage and monetize
data feeds at scale. To achieve delegated governance and distribute grants in a
fully decentralized way, the incentives and processes of the participants are
reconciled through the governance, security, and value capture utilities of the
API3 token and its powers in the API3 DAO.

## DAO primary migration proposal #17 (Oct 2026)

With the
[DAO primary migration proposal #17](https://ivory-general-mouse-678.mypinata.cloud/ipfs/bafybeihrqolxtycbb4oadz5phdvrk6d2m3z6etfcj663mon242qeox26dq)
staking rewards end and governance moves to Snapshot. The DAO contracts stay on
chain indefinitely and remain ownerless. The **DAO dashboard** stays online
until the end of March 2028 with a deprecation notice pointing to the Snapshot
space, and only unstake, withdraw and undelegate remain available on it. Stakers
will be reminded to unstake and withdraw before then. The **DAO tracker** stays
up over the same period as the record of the old DAO, its votes, positions and
rewards. After March 2028 both are retired and we publish step-by-step Etherscan
instructions for calling scheduleUnstake, unstake and withdraw on the pool
directly.

- See the [Guide: Unstake and Withdraw](/overview/guide-unstake-withdraw) for
  help using the DAO Dashboard to withdraw tokens.
- See [Staking Tokens > Claiming rewards](/members/staking#_3-claim-rewards) to
  further understand "Claiming rewards".
